Everything you need to know before getting started.

Most sites are delivered in 5–7 working days once I have your details. Starter sites can be done in 3 days. Premium packages with location pages and SEO content take 7–10 days. I'll give you a clear timeline before we start — no vague "a few weeks" from me.
I deploy on fast, free hosting (Vercel or Netlify) so you're live immediately at no extra cost. If you need a custom domain (like yourbusiness.com), I'll guide you — they're typically £10–15/year in the UK or $12–15/year in the US. I handle the full setup.
Every site I build is mobile-first by default — meaning I design for phones first, then scale to desktop. Over 70% of your customers visit on a phone. I test every site on iPhone, Android, tablet and desktop before delivery.
Absolutely — redesigns are one of my most common projects. I'll keep what's working (your content, Google ranking, brand) and rebuild everything that isn't — design, speed, structure, and mobile experience. No need to start from zero.
